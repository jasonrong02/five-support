Five support

We’ll help you find the next move.

Quick answers for gameplay, ads, privacy choices, and technical issues in Five for iPhone.

Learn the rules again

Open the teaching panel from the Five home screen to replay the guided five-move tutorial at any time.

Undo and restart

Solo and Two Players include two free successful undos per game. Later undos and active-game restarts may offer an optional rewarded video. If no video is available or completed, the board stays unchanged.

Manage advertising privacy

In regions where advertising choices are required, Five shows a Privacy control that reopens the consent form. You can also review the full privacy policy.

Report a problem

Include your iPhone model, iOS version, the game mode, and a short description of what happened. Email yuanjierong02@gmail.com.
