Let’s get your delivery back in the air.

How do the controls work?

Hold anywhere on the flight screen to rise and release to descend. Air currents change the plane’s path. Rain increases moisture, while warm golden wind helps the paper dry.

Why did my route restart?

A collision or a fully soaked paper plane ends the current delivery. Tap Try Again to restart the route. If an interstitial ad is unavailable or consent is incomplete, the restart continues without waiting for an ad.

Where is progress stored?

Tutorial completion and delivered-route progress are stored locally on your iPhone. Paper Plane Messenger does not require an account. Deleting the app normally removes this local progress, subject to iOS backup behavior.

How do I review privacy choices?

Open Settings in the app. Where Google requires ongoing consent controls, a Privacy Options button is shown. You can always read the Paper Plane Messenger privacy policy.
